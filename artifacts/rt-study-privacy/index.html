Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Respiratory Therapy Study Program processes, why it is needed, and which service providers are involved.

Effective and last updated: September 15, 2026

Information this app processes

The app processes study activity needed to provide quizzes, exams, history, and progress tracking. This includes selected topics, session type, question identifiers, answer selections, correct or incorrect results, scores, session status, and creation or completion times.

The service also processes ordinary technical request information needed to deliver and protect the app, such as request identifiers, request paths, response status, IP address, browser or device user agent, and response timing.

Information this app does not request

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, login credentials, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifier, payment information, or personal medical records. Respiratory therapy material is educational content and is not collected as personal health information.

How information is used

Study activity is used only to run study sessions, grade answers, calculate scores, show history, and summarize learning progress. The app does not sell personal information or use study activity for targeted advertising.

Storage and retention

Study sessions and progress are stored in the app’s hosted database. The current version does not have user accounts, so records are not tied to a name or email address. Records may remain stored while the service operates unless they are removed as part of service maintenance.

Service providers and sharing

Replit hosts the web application, API, and database and therefore processes the data and connection information required to operate the service. Google Fonts may receive standard connection information, such as IP address and user agent, when the Inter typeface is loaded.

The app does not include advertising SDKs or dedicated analytics, Firebase, Sentry, or crash-reporting SDKs. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS in production. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored information, but no internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Do not enter patient information or other sensitive personal information into the app.

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for respiratory therapy students and professionals. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You may stop using the app at any time. Because the current version does not use accounts or collect contact details, it may not be possible to identify a particular person’s study records without the applicable session identifier.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app practices or service providers change. The date above will be revised when that happens. For privacy questions or requests, contact the app publisher using the support contact shown on the app’s Google Play listing.

Have questions?

For privacy questions or requests, contact the app publisher using the support contact shown on the app’s Google Play listing.
